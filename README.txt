DARKVEILUM website starter

FILES
- index.html: website structure
- style.css: dark premium responsive design
- script.js: product display, category filters, cart and WhatsApp order
- images/: put your product photos here

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. In script.js, replace WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "212600000000" with your real WhatsApp Business number (digits only, international format).
2. Replace the sample products and prices in the products array with your real products and prices.
3. Add your product photos to images/ using these exact names, or change the image paths in script.js:
   hoodie.jpg
   tshirt.jpg
   bucket-hat.jpg
   gym-tank.jpg
4. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js and the images folder in the same repository root.
5. GitHub Pages is static hosting. This starter uses browser localStorage for the cart and WhatsApp for order messages. It does not include a database, secure online payment or order-management backend.

The sample prices and WhatsApp number are placeholders, not confirmed business information.
